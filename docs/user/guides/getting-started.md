# Getting Started with IvyArc

Welcome to IvyArc! This guide will help you get started with your new account and understand the key features of our authentication and user management system.

## 📋 What is IvyArc?

IvyArc is a comprehensive authentication and authorization platform that provides:

- **Secure User Authentication** - Login with username/email and password
- **Role-Based Access Control** - Different permission levels for different users
- **User Profile Management** - Manage your personal information and preferences
- **Session Management** - Control your active sessions across devices
- **Audit Logging** - Track your account activity for security

## 🚀 Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Registration

1. Visit [https://ivyarc.pro/register](https://ivyarc.pro/register)
2. Fill in the required information:
   - **Username**: Choose a unique username (3-30 characters)
   - **Email**: Your valid email address
   - **Password**: Create a strong password (see requirements below)
   - **First Name**: Your first name
   - **Last Name**: Your last name
3. Click "Create Account"

### Password Requirements
Your password must contain:
- ✅ At least 8 characters
- ✅ One uppercase letter (A-Z)
- ✅ One lowercase letter (a-z)
- ✅ One number (0-9)
- ✅ One special character (!@#$%^&*)

### Step 2: Email Verification

1. Check your email inbox for a verification message
2. Click the verification link in the email
3. Your account will be activated and ready to use

**Note**: The verification link expires after 24 hours. If it expires, you can request a new one from the login page.

## 🔐 Logging In

### Standard Login

1. Visit [https://ivyarc.pro/login](https://ivyarc.pro/login)
2. Enter your **username or email** and **password**
3. Click "Sign In"

### Login Options
- **Remember Me**: Keep you logged in for 30 days (on trusted devices only)
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: Additional security layer (if enabled)

### Forgot Your Password?

1. Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Follow the link to create a new password

## 👤 Managing Your Profile

### Updating Personal Information

1. Navigate to **Profile** in the main menu
2. Click "Edit Profile"
3. Update your information:
   - First and Last Name
   - Email address (requires verification)
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Profile picture (optional)
4. Click "Save Changes"

### Email Address Changes
When you change your email address:
1. You'll receive verification emails at both old and new addresses
2. Your old email remains active until new email is verified
3. Click the verification link in your new email
4. Your email address will be updated

## 🔒 Security Settings

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **Security** in the main menu
2. Click "Change Password"
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter your new password (twice for confirmation)
5. Click "Update Password"

**Security Note**: Changing your password will log you out of all other devices for security.

### Session Management

View and manage your active sessions:

1. Navigate to **Sessions** in the main menu
2. See all active sessions with details:
   - Device type and browser
   - IP address and location
   - Login time
   - Last activity time

### Ending Sessions
- **End This Session**: Logout from current device
- **End Session**: Logout from a specific device
- **End All Other Sessions**: Keep current session, logout everywhere else
- **End All Sessions**: Logout from all devices (including current)

## 🔍 Activity Monitoring

### Viewing Your Activity Log

1. Go to **Activity Log** in the main menu
2. Review different types of activities:
   - **Login Events**: Successful and failed login attempts
   - **Profile Changes**: Updates to your personal information
   - **Security Events**: Password changes, session events
   - **Permission Changes**: Role or permission updates

### Understanding Activity Entries

Each log entry shows:
- **Timestamp**: When the event occurred
- **Event Type**: What happened
- **IP Address**: Where it happened from
- **Device Info**: Browser and device details
- **Status**: Success or failure

### Security Alerts
Watch for suspicious activities:
- ⚠️ Logins from new locations
- ⚠️ Multiple failed login attempts
- ⚠️ Password reset requests you didn't make
- ⚠️ Sessions from unknown devices

## 🛡️ Security Best Practices

### Account Security
- **Use a Strong Password**: Follow the password requirements
- **Keep Email Secure**: Your email is used for account recovery
- **Monitor Sessions**: Regularly check active sessions
- **Review Activity**: Check your activity log periodically
- **Logout When Done**: Always logout on shared computers

### Password Tips
- **Use Unique Passwords**: Don't reuse passwords from other sites
- **Use a Password Manager**: Store passwords securely
- **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**: When available (coming soon)
- **Change Passwords Regularly**: Update every 3-6 months

### Recognizing Phishing
- **Official Emails**: We only send emails from @ivyarc.pro
- **Secure Links**: Always check URLs start with https://ivyarc.pro
- **Never Share Passwords**: We'll never ask for your password via email
- **Report Suspicious Emails**: Contact support if you receive suspicious emails
